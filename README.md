# DealLens

DealLens is a research desk for people evaluating a business acquisition. It helps buyers, brokers, and deal teams compare a target website with a peer, review directional traffic estimates, and turn the findings into focused questions for the seller.

Research can be saved with its sources and periods, then explored through an evidence-linked brief and a Crusoe-powered copilot. DealLens is designed to support due diligence conversations—not to provide a fraud score, an investment recommendation, or a substitute for the seller’s first-party data.

Visit [DealLens](https://lensdeal.replit.app/).
