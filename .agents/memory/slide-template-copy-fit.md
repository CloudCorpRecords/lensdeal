---
name: Slide template copy fit
description: A reusable caution when adapting a compact slide template to real presentation copy.
---

When copying a compact slide template, treat its sample copy as a layout demonstration rather than a capacity guarantee. Longer approved copy needs wider and taller text frames at a legible body size; replacing strings alone can overflow even if the slide looks sparse.

**Why:** A template’s short card headings validated, but real buyer and pricing sentences needed several lines. Expanding height alone still left repeated overflow; adjusting width, size, and line spacing together resolved it.

**How to apply:** Budget body text with the slide validator’s glyph and line-height rules before batch-editing template cards, then run validation once on the whole deck.
